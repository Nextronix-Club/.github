# Nextronix – Robotics Club of UTD CSVTU

Welcome to **Nextronix**, the official Robotics Club of **University Teaching Department, CSVTU** under **Programmers' Paradise Technical Society**. We are a community of passionate students, innovators, and creators exploring the world of robotics, embedded systems, and automation technologies.

## About Us

Nextronix is dedicated to:
- Designing and building robots for learning and competition
- Conducting workshops, hackathons, and hands-on training
- Exploring technologies like IoT, AI, ML, microcontrollers, and automation
- Encouraging open-source collaboration and knowledge sharing

We believe in learning by doing — and turning ideas into impactful projects.

## Our Mission

To bring together students who are passionate about robotics and technology, and help them learn, build, and grow through hands-on experiences and teamwork.

## 🚀 Our Activities

- 🤖 Robot Design & Prototyping  
- 🔬 Research & Development in Robotics and AI  
- 🛠️ Workshops, Tech Talks, and Demos  
- 🏆 Participation in National Competitions  
- 💡 Open-source Projects and Community Contributions

## 📁 Projects

Check out our repositories for:
- Line followers, obstacle avoiders, and autonomous bots  
- Arduino and Raspberry Pi-based automation projects  
- Machine learning applications in robotics  
- Custom mechanical designs and CAD models
